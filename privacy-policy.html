ScanLeaf 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

中文 English

核心承诺：ScanLeaf 的全部功能——拍摄、扫描、图像处理、文字识别（OCR）、文档转换——均在您的设备本地完成。您的任何文档内容都不会上传到我们的服务器，因为我们根本没有服务器。

一、我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何您的个人信息或文档内容。

  • 不要求注册账号，无需登录；
  • 不嵌入任何统计、分析或广告 SDK；
  • 不进行任何形式的用户跟踪或行为分析；
  • 您扫描、识别、转换的文档仅保存在您的设备本地（以及您主动选择使用的系统分享功能中）。

二、设备权限的使用

为实现扫描功能，App 会在您授权后使用以下系统权限：

  • 相机：用于拍摄文档页面。仅在您主动使用拍摄功能时调用；
  • 照片图库：仅当您主动从相册导入图片，或主动将结果保存到相册时访问。

您可以随时在系统「设置」中更改这些授权。拒绝授权不影响 App 内不依赖该权限的其他功能。

三、内购与付款

App 内提供付费功能，通过 Apple App Store 内购系统完成支付。所有付款信息由 Apple 处理，我们无法接触也无法存储您的支付账户、银行卡等任何付款信息。退款等支付相关事宜请遵循 Apple 的相关条款与政策。

四、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 14 周岁以下儿童进行定向收集，也绝不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。由于我们根本不收集任何信息，儿童与其他用户受到同等保护。

五、第三方服务

本 App 不嵌入任何第三方分析、广告或追踪服务。您通过系统分享面板将文档分享至其他应用（如邮件、云盘）时，该传输行为由 iOS 系统与目标应用处理，受其各自隐私政策约束。

六、政策变更

若本政策发生重大变更，我们会在本页面发布更新并修订生效日期。由于我们不收集用户联系方式，无法单独通知您，建议您定期查阅本页面。

如您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：vito7777777@163.com

ScanLeaf Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Our core promise: every feature of ScanLeaf — camera capture, scanning, image processing, OCR, and document conversion — runs entirely on your device. None of your document content is ever uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information or document content.

  • No account registration or login is required;
  • No analytics, statistics, or advertising SDKs are embedded;
  • No user tracking or behavioral profiling of any kind;
  • Documents you scan, recognize, or convert are stored only on your device (and in destinations you explicitly choose via system sharing).

2. Device Permissions

To enable scanning, the App requests the following system permissions only with your consent:

  • Camera: used to photograph document pages, invoked only when you actively use the capture feature;
  • Photo Library: accessed only when you import images from your library or explicitly save results to it.

You may revoke these permissions at any time in system Settings. Denying a permission does not affect features that do not rely on it.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

Paid features are offered through the Apple App Store's in-app purchase system. All payment information is processed by Apple; we never have access to and never store your payment account, card numbers, or any billing details. Refunds and payment matters are governed by Apple's terms and policies.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14 and we will never knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no information at all, children receive exactly the same protection as every other user.

5. Third-Party Services

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are embedded in the App. When you share documents to other apps (e.g., Mail, cloud drives) via the iOS share sheet, that transfer is handled by iOS and the destination app under their respective privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We will post any material changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you individually — please review this page periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at vito7777777@163.com.
